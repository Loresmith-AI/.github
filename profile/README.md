# LoreSmith

**Session prep for game masters that remembers your whole campaign.**

[![LoreSmith: session prep for game masters](https://loresmith.ai/og-card.png)](https://loresmith.ai)

For game masters running any tabletop RPG: LoreSmith reads the sourcebooks, notes and session recaps you already have, and turns them into a campaign you can ask questions of. Every fact it pulls out is shown to you for approval first. Nothing enters your campaign because a model was confident about it.

## What it does

- **Reads what you already have.** Upload PDFs, notes, DOCX files and images, and LoreSmith finds the characters, places and factions in them.
- **Asks before it remembers.** Each fact it finds waits for you to approve, edit or reject it.
- **Plans against what was actually run.** Session recaps sit alongside your approved facts, so the next session's plan fits the party you really have.
- **Keeps your players in the picture.** Share handouts, maps and lore when you are ready, and players' answers come only from what you have shared.
- **Works at the table.** A table view for the session itself, on a laptop or a phone.
